Round 1: review of SDN_DDoS_Paper_Revision0.pdf

Uploaded on 22 Nov 2026; Deep Review document ID 98a4c3e3-c36c-4656-82e2-f9f7011215f2.
Context given to Deep Review: "IEEE conference paper (course research project) on ML-based DDoS detection and mitigation in SDN, evaluated on a Mininet/Ryu testbed. Please check the mathematical formulation, the experimental methodology and baselines, the statistical validity of the results, and whether the claims are supported by the evidence."

New experiments and analyses in this revision

  • Fresh seed 3 with the frozen final configuration: one detection-only and one mitigation session (shuffled attack order, 60 s gaps)
  • Calibration session repeated at the 25 Mbit/s test capacity (sensitivity analysis)
  • Offline ablation: Levels 2+3 without the flow model, refitted on the calibration session
  • Offline: fast-path parity for all baselines, survivorship-free (penalised) latency, per-role false-positive rates
  • Live-attribution diagnosis per attacker and episode (diagnose_attribution.py) and carry-over accounting (rules active at onset)
